About

Hakuba Snow Compass · Hakuba Valley, Nagano, Japan

Hakuba Snow Compass is a free, mobile-first web app for skiers and snowboarders visiting Hakuba Valley. Every morning it answers one question: which of the valley's 10 resorts is the best bet today?

How it works

  • Live forecasts. Wind, gusts, snowfall, temperature and freezing level are fetched every hour for the summit and base of each resort.
  • Terrain profiles. Each resort has a profile: vertical, aspect, tree cover, wind exposure and how much snow it usually catches.
  • Lift status. Live lift operations are checked during the day, so a closed mountain drops down the list.
  • Backcountry Mode. Wind by elevation, weather red flags, gate rules and direct links to the Japan Avalanche Network bulletin. It is weather data only, never an avalanche forecast.

Who runs it

An independent developer based in Japan who wants first-time visitors to spend less time guessing and more time skiing. The app is not affiliated with Hakuba Valley, any resort, or the Japan Avalanche Network.

How we keep it free

Some links in the app go to booking partners for tours, gear rentals and airport transfers. If you book through them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Partners never pay for a better ranking. The resort scores come from the forecast and terrain data alone.

Data sources

Weather data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Lift and shuttle information from official Hakuba Valley sources. Rankings are automated estimates. Always check official lift status before you travel.
