Our rules

  • Always labelled. Every paid listing says "Sponsored". We never present a paid listing as our own recommendation.
  • Rankings can't be bought. The daily resort ranking is calculated from forecasts and terrain only. Sponsorship never changes a score.
  • Safety first. We don't list off-piste or backcountry routes, and we don't accept listings that encourage skiing outside designated gates.
  • No banners or pop-ups. Listings are simple cards that match the app, so the app stays fast on the mountain.
  • We may decline listings that don't fit a ski trip to Hakuba.

About our audience

The app launched in November 2026 and is written in English for international visitors, mainly from Australia, Asia, North America and Europe. We are a new, small site and we don't inflate numbers: ask us and we'll share our current visitor figures before you decide.
