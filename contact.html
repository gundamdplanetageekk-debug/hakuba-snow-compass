Good reasons to write

  • Something in the app is wrong or out of date, such as a lift, a shuttle time or a rule.
  • An idea that would make your Hakuba trip easier.
  • Partnerships with tour operators, guides, rental shops and accommodation.

Not for emergencies

We can't help in an emergency. In Japan call 110 (police) or 119 (fire and ambulance). For lift tickets, bookings or lost property, contact the resort or the booking partner directly.
