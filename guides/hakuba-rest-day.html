Rest Day in Hakuba: 7 Things to Do Off the Slopes

Strong wind on the summits, rain at the base, or legs that need a day off: a rest day is part of a good Hakuba trip. Here are the options, from half a day to a full day out. Check opening hours and seasonal timetables before you go.

1. Snow monkeys & Zenko-ji Temple

Full day · guided tours pick up in Hakuba in winter

Wild macaques bathing in a hot spring at Jigokudani is Nagano's most famous sight. It is about two hours from Hakuba by road, and the last part is a forest walk on a snowy trail, so wear proper boots. The easiest way is a guided day tour with pickup in Hakuba, which usually pairs the monkeys with Zenko-ji Temple in Nagano.

2. An onsen afternoon

2–3 hours · in Hakuba or Omachi Onsen

Hot springs are everywhere in the valley, from small public baths in Happo to the onsen town of Omachi in the south. Wash before you get in, keep your towel out of the water and keep your voice down. If you have tattoos, check the policy first: many baths still refuse visible tattoos.

3. Matsumoto Castle

Full day · about 2 hours each way by train

One of Japan's original wooden castles, in a walkable city with old merchant streets, cafés and soba. Take the JR Oito Line from Hakuba Station towards Matsumoto; check the timetable, because trains are infrequent.

4. Nagano city

Half to full day · express bus from Hakuba

Zenko-ji is one of Japan's most important Buddhist temples, a short walk or bus ride from Nagano Station. Around it you will find shops, sake and local food. The Hakuba–Nagano express bus makes it an easy trip; book a seat in peak weeks.

5. Mountain views without skiing

2–3 hours · clear days

On a clear but windy day, when the upper lifts are on hold, a lower gondola can still give you big views of the Northern Alps. Iwatake, on the east side of the valley, is known for its panorama. Ask at the ticket office about one-ride tickets for non-skiers.

6. Snowshoeing or another guided activity

Half day

A snowshoe walk through the forest is a gentle way to enjoy fresh snow without lifts. Go with a local guide and stay out of avalanche terrain. You can also browse lessons, tours and activities in Hakuba.

7. A slow village day

Any length

Sleep in, get your boots fitted, find a good coffee in Happo or Echoland, book the next few dinners, and plan tomorrow. Check the app in the afternoon: after 14:00 it switches to tomorrow's ranking.

Lift pass tip: multi-day Hakuba Valley passes give you a few days of slack (for example, a 2-day pass is valid for any two days within four), so a rest day doesn't have to waste a ski day.
